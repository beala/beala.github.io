FIRMWARE IN FLUX

A lab for crazy ideas.

FluxCore is a MeshCore fork for trying things. New features, and experiments that might turn into something useful.

The goal is simple: move quickly and stay compatible with vanilla MeshCore. Keep using the network that’s already there. Give new ideas somewhere to run.

/Features in flux

A second chance for missed packets. If your radio doesn’t hear a repeater forward its message, it sends the packet once more. Nodes that already have it ignore the duplicate. Retries

One identity, several radios. Keep one at home and carry another. Pick a main radio; backups hold their replies when they hear it answer. This coordinates replies—it doesn’t sync your message history. Takeover

Find the radio you put somewhere sensible. Make it beep from an authorized radio. Two supported LR2021 radios can also measure the distance between them, with a direct link in the supported 902–928 MHz band. Find me

Also: packet parser fixes, improved key generation, radio diagnostics, and opt-in experimental encryption. All the changes.
